Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 20% annual discount work? ▼
When you choose Yearly Billing, you receive an immediate 20% discount on the entire 12-month period compared to month-to-month billing. For instance, on the Royal Gold plan, you save Rs. 20,400 per year, enjoying uninterrupted healthcare privileges with zero inflation price increases.
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan anytime? ▼
Yes, absolutely! You can upgrade your membership tier at any time from your client dashboard with prorated adjustments. Monthly memberships can be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle with zero cancellation fees.
What is your 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee policy? ▼
We stand behind our 5-star medical excellence. If you or your pet are unsatisfied with our hospital services within your first 30 days of subscribing, simply contact our concierge desk for a prompt, 100% full refund with no hard feelings.
Which payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept all major Pakistani and global payment gateways: Visa, MasterCard, UnionPay credit/debit cards, Raast Instant Pay, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Direct Bank Transfer (IBFT), as well as Cash / POS card swipe directly at our clinic reception.
Can I cover multiple pets under a single plan? ▼
Each plan is specifically customized for one pet's unique medical records and vaccination schedules. However, multi-pet households receive an automatic 15% discount on the second pet and 20% on the third pet when registered under the same parent account.
