Our Specialized Medical Facilities

At VetiMedicos, every department is custom-engineered for maximum clinical precision, humane comfort, and rapid emergency intervention. Discover our state-of-the-art facilities detailed below.

Critical Care 🟢 24/7 LIVE ON DUTY

24/7 Emergency & Trauma Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Immediate life-saving emergency medical interventions for acute vehicular trauma, internal hemorrhages, toxic ingestions, severe respiratory failure, and cardiac emergencies with round-the-clock rapid response.

  • Continuous Pressurized Oxygen Therapy Cages & Nebulizers
  • Mindray Multiparameter Patient Vital Signs Monitors (ECG, SpO2, NIBP)
  • Emergency Blood Transfusion Unit & Fresh Frozen Plasma Storage
  • Crash Cart Defibrillator, Syringe Infusion Pumps & Suction

Executive Surgical & Orthopedic Suites (OT-1 & OT-2)

Positive-pressure, HEPA-filtered sterile surgical theaters for complex soft-tissue surgeries, orthopedic bone fracture plating (TPLO), neurosurgery, thoracic operations, and minimally invasive laparoscopy.

⚡ Surgical Equipment & Anesthesia Safety
  • Dräger Isoflurane / Sevoflurane Inhalation Anesthesia with Ventilator
  • Stryker Orthopedic Bone Power Saws & Dynamic Compression Plating
  • High-Definition Rigid Laparoscopic Tower for Keyhole Surgery
  • Harmonic Ultrasonic Scalpel & Bipolar Electro-Cautery

Diagnostic Radiology 🟢 REAL-TIME SCANNING

Digital Radiography (X-Ray) & Color Doppler Sonography

Instant low-dose digital imaging for structural fracture analysis, pulmonary evaluations, and live multi-frequency echocardiography to detect cardiac murmurs and abdominal organ pathologies.

⚡ Advanced Imaging Technologies
  • Canon Flat-Panel High-Frequency Digital X-Ray (Instant High-Res)
  • GE LOGIQ Color Doppler Ultrasound with Micro-Convex & Cardiac Probes
  • Digital Contrast Studies & Foreign Body Gastrointestinal Transit
  • DICOM Cloud Archiving: Instant report delivery to Pet Parent app

Location / Wing Ground Floor — Imaging Suite 104

Central Lab 🟢 15-MIN TEST RESULTS

In-House Clinical Pathology & Molecular Blood Lab

Fully automated laboratory providing real-time hematology, liver/kidney biochemistries, electrolyte balances, urinalysis, and rapid PCR snap testing for Parvovirus, Distemper, FeLV, and FIV.

⚡ Laboratory Diagnostic Analyzers
  • Automated Laser Hematology CBC Analyzer (26 Parameters)
  • Dry-Chemistry Analyzer (ALT, BUN, Creatinine, Lipase, Bilirubin)
  • Blood Gas, Acid-Base & Electrolyte (Na+, K+, Cl-) Profiler
  • High-Power Phase-Contrast Microscope for Skin Scraping & Cytology

Specialized Canine Wellness, Vaccination & Pediatric OPD
